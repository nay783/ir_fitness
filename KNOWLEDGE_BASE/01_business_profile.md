## The Four Ecosystem Pillars
1. **High-Performance Gym Floor:** Heavy strength equipment, progressive conditioning, and dedicated muscle-group biomechanics.
2. **IR Karate Academy (KSI):** Kimura Shukokai International (KSI) martial arts training, youth discipline classes, and the signature *Tatami Friendship* tournament series.
3. **IR Fitness Nutrition Shop:** Dedicated supplement distribution carrying premier sports brands and proprietary products (*Whey Protein Baunilha IR* and *Creatina IR Fitness Nutrition*).
4. **Signature Fitness & Music Events:** Experiential mass workouts, notably the *Music & Gym Experience*, pairing high-intensity training with resident DJs, supplement tastings, and community socialization.
