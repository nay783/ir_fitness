# 02. Brand Identity & Design System

## Brand Core & Positioning
A premier high-performance fitness center where physical conditioning, martial discipline, and music-driven social experiences converge. At IR Fitness Gym, training is framed not as routine exertion, but as a repeatable personal victory.

## Slogans & Taglines
- **Primary Bio Tagline:** *"Especialistas em alta performance."*
- **Brand Slogan:** *"Onde cada treino é uma vitória."*
- **Action Mantras:** *"Venha superar seus limites."* | *"Go hard or go home!"*

## Tone of Voice & Personality
- **Energetic & Assertive:** High octane, confident, results-focused.
- **Inspirational & Supportive:** Celebrating milestones, encouraging consistency through winter and off-seasons.
- **Bilingual Fluency:** Authentic Mozambican Portuguese (PT-MZ) paired with bold English athletic idioms.

## Visual Design Tokens (Crimson Performance Theme)
- **Primary Canvas (`--background`):** `#080808` (Deep Charcoal Asphalt)
- **Card Surface (`--surface`):** `#141414`
- **Elevated Hover (`--surface-hover`):** `#1c1c1c`
- **Brand Accent (`--brand-red`):** `#E11D48` (Athletic Crimson / Hexagon Red)
- **Accent Glow (`--brand-red-hover`):** `#F43F5E`
- **Brand Red Muted (`--brand-red-muted`):** `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.15)`
- **Typography Tokens:**
  - **Display / Headlines:** `Bebas Neue` / `Barlow Condensed` (Uppercase, tight tracking)
  - **Body / Interface:** `Inter` / `Manrope`
  - **Numeric & Badges:** `JetBrains Mono`
